Subject: Proposal to rebuild and secure the Akright City website

Dear info@akrightcity.com, President Simon Kaggwa, Hon Allan Kiwumulo, and Hon Shyaka Mbanda,

We are pleased to submit Webstar's proposal to rebuild the Akright City website as a modern, responsive, secure, and easily maintainable platform for the Akright City Residents Association.

Our review identified several opportunities to improve the current site: inconsistent mobile navigation, multiple active hero slides, missing security headers, an unprotected contact form, dynamic news links without clear validation, missing sitemap and metadata, and limited external-link protections. The proposed rebuild addresses these issues while preserving all existing public content, village pages, reports, directory entries, news, FAQs, gallery items, and community information.

The rebuilt site will include:

- A fully responsive public website
- A role-protected admin content management system
- Secure contact form handling with validation, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and spam controls
- Legacy URL redirects so existing links continue to work
- Security headers and safer external/embedded content handling
- Sitemap, robots, canonical, and Open Graph metadata
- Automated tests and a documented handover

Our fixed fee for the rebuild is **UGX 3,500,000**, with payment terms of **50% on contract signing, 40% on staging approval, and 10% on launch and handover**. Optional maintenance and support after launch is available at **UGX 150,000 per month**.

Delivery will take **six weeks** from signed contract and complete handover of content, domain, hosting, and current webmaster access. The fee includes fourteen days of post-launch bug-fix support.

To proceed, we kindly request:

1. Confirmation of the proposal and fee.
2. An introduction to the current webmaster or hosting administrator.
3. Confirmation of the content owner who will provide the content freeze and approvals.
4. A short kickoff meeting to agree the content and access handover.

The attached proposal provides the full scope, timeline, payment schedule, and acceptance criteria.
